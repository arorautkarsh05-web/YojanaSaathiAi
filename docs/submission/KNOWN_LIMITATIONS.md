## 1. Explicit Current Boundaries & Non-Claims

1. **Non-Claimant Boundary (We Guide, We Do Not Sanction):**  
   YojanaSaathi AI is an evidence-grounded intelligence and preparation assistant. It does **not** process, sanction, or disburse government payments. The sovereign power to approve schemes remains exclusively with designated government authorities (Tehsildars, District Agriculture Officers, Bank Branch Managers).
2. **Current Geographic Scope (Uttar Pradesh Only):**  
   While our architecture is designed for pan-India scalability, our verified statutory rules, district clustering, and agro-climatic zoning currently cover all **75 districts of Uttar Pradesh**. Rules for other states (e.g., Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Punjab) are not yet fully gazetted in the seeder.
3. **Core Scheme Catalogue (6 Active Schemes):**  
   The platform focuses on 6 flagship schemes: PM-KISAN, PMFBY, PMKSY-PDMC, KCC, UP Krishak Durghatna Kalyan Yojana, and SMAM Machinery. Other niche state welfare schemes (e.g. Free Boring Scheme, Gaushala grants) require ongoing statutory gazetting.
4. **Third-Party API Integration (No Direct Sovereign Portal Write Access):**  
   YojanaSaathi does not have direct read/write API access into restricted government databases (such as UP Bhulekh or PFMS). Citizens must enter their land area and crop details manually or via conversational intake.
5. **DPDP Compliance Notice:**  
   Our platform implements data minimization and consent mechanisms aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. However, formal statutory certification requires external legal audit.

## 2. Engineering Roadmap (Post-Hackathon Expansion)

- **Phase 1 (Q1 2027):** Direct DigiLocker API integration for instant, consent-based verification of Khatauni land records and Aadhaar e-KYC.
- **Phase 2 (Q2 2027):** Expansion of statutory catalogue from 6 to 25 Central and State schemes covering animal husbandry, fisheries, and horticulture.
- **Phase 3 (Q3 2027):** Voice-first native telephony interface (IVR / WhatsApp audio) for illiterate farmers without smartphones.
- **Phase 4 (Q4 2027):** Pan-India geographic expansion to all 28 states and 8 union territories.
